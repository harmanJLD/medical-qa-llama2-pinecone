How answers are made
  1. Your question is embedded with all-MiniLM-L6-v2.
  2. Pinecone returns the 10 closest passages.
  3. A cross-encoder reranks them and keeps the best 4.
  4. If nothing is relevant, the app says so instead of guessing.
  5. Llama 2 7B writes the answer and cites each page.
